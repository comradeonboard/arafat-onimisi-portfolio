01 About

Making the web feel human again.

I'm Arafat — a designer and developer who cares about the small things: rhythm, contrast, load time, and the quiet moment before a page settles.

Over the past few years I've helped founders and studios turn rough ideas into products people actually enjoy using. I work end to end — from the first sketch to the last line of CSS — and I like keeping the process simple, collaborative and a little bit obsessive.

When I'm not shipping, I'm collecting typefaces I'll never use and redesigning interfaces that were already fine.
